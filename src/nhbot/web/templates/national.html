This is a household (individual) tax burden: state and local property, individual income, general sales, and excise tax collections per capita, divided by income per capita. It deliberately excludes business and corporate taxes, which fall on companies rather than residents. Figures are compiled from U.S. Census Bureau state & local government finance data (fiscal year 2023) and Bureau of Economic Analysis income data (2024), by way of the Tax Foundation's Facts & Figures 2026; we compute the burden ourselves from those underlying per-capita figures, so the method is fully transparent.
