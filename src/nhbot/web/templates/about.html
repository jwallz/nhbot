About NHDataHub

NHDataHub is a side project of a software-engineering resident of New Hampshire. A lot of meaningful political, fiscal, and economic data about the state is scattered across state, local, and independent websites — hard to find, and harder to compare. The idea here is simple: pull the most useful of it into one place, organized around the town you live in and the questions a typical resident actually asks.

Today that means, for each of New Hampshire's 234 cities and towns: property tax rates (both the advertised rate and the equalized rate, so you can compare towns fairly), school spending and enrollment, town and school operating budgets, your state representatives and senator, and your local select board — each with its source cited.

The aim is to stay nonpartisan and let the data speak. Where a figure is an estimate, or a town's numbers simply aren't published yet, the site says so rather than papering over the gap.

If you have comments to share, a correction to flag, or a feature you'd like to see, please fill out the contact form.
