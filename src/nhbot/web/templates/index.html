New Hampshire has one of the most decentralized, hyper-local governance structures in the U.S.—with 221 towns, 13 cities, 400 House representatives serving tiny districts (~3,300 residents each), and stark differences in tax rates (e.g., zero income/sales tax putting the entire weight on local property tax). NHDataHub is a nonpartisan, open resource for all things New Hampshire — town by town. The data does the talking: budgets, taxes, schools, and more, for every one of the state's 234 municipalities.
