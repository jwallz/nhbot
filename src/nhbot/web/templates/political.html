How this is built: each seated legislator counts equally — every state representative from a town's district (including any floterial district it shares) plus its state senator — and the color reflects that group's split between the two major parties. A town with one lone representative therefore reads as fully one color; the tooltip's tally is the ground truth behind every shade. Independents are counted in the tally but don't tilt the color.
