# NHbot Phase 0 Spike — Findings

**Date:** 2026-08-21  **Tax year:** 2025  **Scope:** DRA tax rates + equalization ratios → equalized (full-value) rates for all 234 NH municipalities.

## Result

Pulled the authoritative NH DRA workbooks for tax year 2025, joined them cleanly across all 234 municipalities, and computed the equalized tax rate. Output: `phase0/nh_2025_equalized_rates.csv` (260 rows: 234 municipalities + 25 unincorporated places + Penacook village district), generated by `phase0/compute_equalized_rates.py`.

The spike confirms the project's core thesis. Reordering towns by **equalized** rate instead of the **advertised** rate moves **126 of 234 towns by more than 20 rank positions** (max shift 162). The advertised number is genuinely misleading for cross-town comparison, and the equalization correction is the differentiator it was assumed to be.

Illustrative:

| Town | Advertised total | Equalization ratio | Equalized (simple) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claremont | 30.54 | 77.12% | 23.55 |
| Alton | 13.50 | 57.00% | 7.70 |
| New Castle | 5.73 | 63.92% | 3.66 |
| Berlin | 33.60 | 79.00% | 26.54 |
| Hart's Location | 2.62 | 88.50% | 2.32 |

Alton and New Castle (low-ratio lakefront/coastal towns) fall sharply once equalized; Berlin and Claremont stay high. Statewide equalized (simple method): min 2.32, median 14.25, mean 13.92, max 26.54.

**Verified:** the 2025 tax rates and the 2025 equalization ratios are both published and vintage-aligned (DRA releases each May's equalization study for the prior tax year; the 2025 study is out).

## Open methodology question — must resolve before this becomes a published number

The equalized rate is computed **two ways**, and they diverge:

- `equalized_rate_simple = total_advertised_rate × (ratio/100)` — the convention most NH town assessing offices and third-party sites cite.
- `equalized_rate_rigorous = net_tax_commitment / (net_valuation / (ratio/100)) × 1000` — closer to DRA's own "full value tax rate" definition (taxes raised ÷ equalized valuation).

Divergence across the 234: median |diff| 0.15, mean 0.20, **max 1.51**. Most of the gap is veterans'-credit netting (commitment is net of credits, the advertised rate is not). **Concord is a reversed outlier** (simple 17.91 vs rigorous 18.67) — its county-table net valuation is smaller than the base its advertised rate was struck on, almost certainly a village-district (Penacook) valuation-overlay effect. Flag: village districts overlaying a host municipality need explicit modeling; don't trust a blind name-join for the ~30 towns with village/precinct districts.

**These differences are large enough to change rankings and to matter for the flagship comparison tool.** Neither column should be presented as "the" equalized rate until it's validated against DRA's own published figure.

**Blocker on validating now:** DRA's *2025* "Comparison of Full Value Tax Rates" is not yet published (only through 2024). 

**Recommended next step:** run this same code against **2024** inputs and diff against DRA's published `2024-comparison-of-full-value-tax-rates-ranking-order.pdf`. Whichever method reproduces DRA's 2024 numbers is the canonical one; lock it, then apply to 2025. Requires downloading the 2024 workbooks (same Akamai-protected DRA host — browser download, not curl).

## Operational notes for the ingest pipeline

- **DRA is behind Akamai bot management.** Plain HTTP clients (`curl`, `requests`) get an "Access Denied" page on its file URLs regardless of user-agent — it fingerprints TLS. Use a **headless browser (Playwright)** for the DRA source module. Other MVP sources (Census API, etc.) are unaffected.
- DRA now publishes these in **Excel**, not just PDF — parse the xlsx, don't scrape PDFs.
- Column positions are stable within a year but header labels carry newlines and a spurious prior-year title cell; parse by position off the known header row, not by fuzzy header match. Re-verify positions each new vintage.
- Canonical ID is still an open design choice (Census GEOID vs DRA codes) — this spike joined on normalized name, which worked for 234/234 but is not durable. Building the GEOID-anchored crosswalk is the right Phase 1 task.
